# Code of conduct

This repository is worked on by one human owner and several AI agents, and it welcomes contributions from other people. The same standards apply to everyone who takes part, whether human or agent.

## Our standards

- Be respectful and constructive. Assume good faith, and say what you mean plainly.
- Give and accept feedback about the work, not the person.
- Show evidence. Claims about what works come with the command that was run and its result.
- Keep discussions relevant to the project, and keep the reasoning in the open so others can follow it.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, discrimination, insults or personal attacks.
- Publishing someone's private information.
- Sexualized language or unwanted sexual attention.
- Spam, or repeatedly pushing a point after it has been decided.
- Misrepresenting work: claiming checks passed when they did not, or changing a check so that it passes.

## Scope

This applies in issues, pull requests, comments and any other space where someone acts on behalf of the project.

## Enforcement

The owners are responsible for enforcing this code of conduct. They may remove or edit comments, close or lock a discussion, and block someone temporarily or permanently, depending on how serious and how repeated the behavior is.

To report a problem, contact an owner (`nguyenquocduyloc` or `NguyenLoc231`, the owner accounts listed in `.github/CODEOWNERS`) through the contact details on their GitHub profile. If you cannot find a way to reach them privately, open a new issue with the **Request a private channel** form, without describing the incident. An owner will answer with a private way to share it. Reports are handled privately and treated with care.
